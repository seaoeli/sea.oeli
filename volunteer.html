Recruitment Information

Monthly Recruitment

Volunteer applications are open the first 10 days of each month

Age Requirements

We welcome youth volunteers aged 13-21

Global Community

Volunteers from anywhere in the world are welcome, provided they are fluent in English.

Remote Volunteering

Most volunteering activities are conducted remotely, allowing you to contribute from anywhere.

Responsibilities

As a volunteer, you will be expected to provide feedback on student assignments and assist with our live online classes. Don't worry if you are unable to attend every lesson or are unsure how to grade assignments. Volunteers will be part of a volunteer group responsible for a class, where responsibilities will be distributed among team members according to availability.
