About SEA OELI

One of SEA OELI’s core beliefs is the joy of learning. Yet, educational inequality still remains a widespread problem faced by many communities all across Southeast Asia. Access to education is often affected by circumstances entirely beyond an individual’s control, meaning that people with the same eagerness and potential to learn may not have the same opportunities to do so. At SEA OELI, we want to change that. We believe that everyone deserves a fair and equal opportunity to learn. And most importantly, that a person’s circumstances should not determine the limits of their future before they have even had the chance to begin.
