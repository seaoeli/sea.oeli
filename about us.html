About SEA OELI

What do we do?

SEA OELI designs and creates our own structured curriculum of English resources. We chose English as our focus because proficiency in a global language can open doors to further educational, professional, and personal opportunities. All our resources are completely free and accessible to any passionate learner, aligning with our mission of bridging education inequality in Southeast Asia.

Our heart

One of SEA OELI’s core beliefs is the joy of learning. Yet, educational inequality remains a widespread problem faced by many communities across Southeast Asia. Access to education is often shaped by circumstances entirely beyond an individual’s control, meaning that people with the same eagerness and potential to learn may not have the same opportunities to do so.

Our mission

At SEA OELI, we want to change that. We believe that everyone deserves a fair and equal opportunity to learn. And most importantly, that a person’s circumstances should not determine the limits of their future before they have even had the chance to begin.
